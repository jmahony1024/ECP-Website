Bio

Electric City Pulse (ECP) is a funk, R&B and rock band based in the Electric City (Peterborough, ON, Canada). They cover an eclectic mix of high energy, fun tunes that get audiences smiling, dancing and having a great time! They may not take themselves too seriously, but they are serious musicians. Several band members play multiple instruments on the same songs, producing a big sound for a six member group. ECP played their first ever show in June 2024 and are now having a ton of fun playing regularly in the Peterborough area and beyond.


The bandmembers are:

Mike - Drums
Chris - Bass, backing vocals
Kathryn - Lead vocals, percussion
Jason - Lead guitar, trumpet, cowbell
Scott - Saxophone, recorder and other wind instruments, guitar, backing vocals
Joe - Lead vocals, guitar, keyboard, harmonica, fiddle, mandolin, slidewhistle, vibraslap
